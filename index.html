Equipment

  • Phone: This will serve as our camera and recording device to capture the slime making process and broadcast it live to our viewers.
  • Tripod: Hold the phone steady so it gives the audience a clear view of what we are doing during the live.
  • Slime-Making Materials: We need these because these are the actual items such as glue, activator and mixing tool needed to perform our activity for our audience.
  • Stable Internet Connection: This is to prevent lag, freezing, or sudden disconnection during the livestream.
  • OBS Studio: This will be used as our streaming software to capture and manage our video and audio sources and broadcast the livestream to YouTube.

SMART Goal

By September 18, 2026, our group will conduct a 30-minute interactive livestream about nonverbal communication for Grade 10 students.

We will present 3 main topics, 3 activities, and 5 real-life examples while planning for 10–20 viewers.

Research Journey !

How our research helped us improve our stream.

1. Starting Point

We started by identifying a common problem: people can easily misunderstand body language when they do not consider the situation.

2. What We Found

Our research showed us that nonverbal communication can include facial expressions, eye contact, gestures, posture, and other movements.

3. What Changed

We changed our activities to make them faster, easier, and more interactive for Grade 10 viewers.

4. Our Final Idea

We decided to combine our research with slime making so viewers can learn about nonverbal communication while watching and participating in a fun activity.
